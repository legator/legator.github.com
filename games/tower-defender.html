Click an empty cell to place the selected tower. Click a tower to inspect it, then upgrade or sell it. Right-click, or long-press on touch, sells a tower.
Sound: every tower type makes its own sound when it hits a creep, creeps pop when killed, and a boss kill is louder. Use the Sound button at the top to turn it off.
Every tower has 4 upgrades. For each one you choose what to improve: damage (+10%), range (+0.3) or reload (10% faster); the 4th upgrade counts double. Support towers improve their power instead of damage. Colored dots under a tower show what you upgraded: red damage, blue range, green reload.
Targeting: single-target towers shoot the creep closest to the exit by default. Before the first upgrade you can switch them to the closest creep, the furthest one, or the one with the lowest health. Pick it in the Selected tab before placing, or on the placed tower.
Creep filter: a tower hits every creep type by default. Before its first upgrade you can set it to hit only one type (for example only Fast, or only Bosses); it then ignores everything else, including splash damage and slows. A colored dot on the tower shows the type it is set to.
Difficulty: Easy is the baseline. Middle gives creeps +50% health and you +10% starting gold, Hard +100% and +25%, Extreme +250% and +50%.
Developer mode (Mode menu) adds a Dev tab: edit tower numbers (price, damage, range, reload, radius, buffs, air/ground), creep health, speed and reward, the damage percentages, game settings, and write your own wave script. Press Run to restart the map with your numbers. Edits are saved in the browser, can be exported and imported as JSON, and are ignored in the other modes.
Mode: Extended (default) lets you choose what each upgrade improves and set targeting and creep filters. Normal is the simple version: one Upgrade button that adds power and range, and every tower hits every creep type. Changing the mode restarts the map.
Each upgrade takes the tower offline for a short time. Each wave gives you 25 s; when the timer runs out the next wave starts on its own. You can launch the next wave early.
Waves come in blocks of 9: eight regular waves, then a boss wave. In the first block each wave brings one creep type (Normal, Fast, Group, Air, Tough, Decoy, Spawner) and the 8th wave mixes them all. Each later block mixes one more type per wave: two types in the second block, three in the third, and so on. A boss wave has only the boss. Bosses come in turn: Normal on wave 9, Fast on 18, Group on 27, Air on 36, Tough on 45, Decoy on 54, and Spawner on 63, the last wave. Bosses pay a lot of gold.
Keys: 1-0 select a tower (the first ten), with a tower inspected, U upgrades its damage, I its range and O its reload, Esc clears the selection, Space launches the next wave, P pauses and resumes. You can place and sell towers while paused.
