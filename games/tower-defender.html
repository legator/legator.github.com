Click an empty cell to place the selected tower. Click a tower to inspect it, then upgrade or sell it. Right-click, or long-press on touch, sells a tower. On a phone, tap an empty cell to preview the tower and its range, tap the same cell again to place it.
Sound: every tower type makes its own sound when it hits a creep, creeps pop when killed, and a boss kill is louder. Use the Sound button at the top to turn it off.
In Extended mode damage (+8% per level), range (+0.25) and reload (8% faster) each have their own 4 levels. All three share one price, which rises as the most-upgraded stat rises: after damage reaches level 2, the next upgrade costs the same for damage, range and reload. The 4th level of a stat counts double. Support towers improve their power instead of damage. Colored dots under a tower show the levels: red damage, blue range, green reload. In Normal mode a tower has one Upgrade button with 4 levels.
Targeting: single-target towers shoot the creep closest to the exit by default. Before the first upgrade you can switch them to the closest creep, the furthest one, or the one with the lowest health. Pick it in the Selected tab before placing, or on the placed tower.
Creep filter: a tower hits every creep type by default. Before its first upgrade you can set it to hit only one type (for example only Fast, or only Bosses); it then ignores everything else, including splash damage and slows. A colored dot on the tower shows the type it is set to.
Early launch: pressing Launch (or Space) before the countdown ends pays gold for every second you skip, 0.4 gold per second on wave 1 and 0.04 more with each wave (scaled by the level's gold bonus). The button shows the amount.
Eras (header menu, on by default): a run starts in the Ancient era with only the first five towers. The Era bar lets you advance for gold; otherwise the next era begins free at wave 19, 37 and 55. While you are ahead of schedule creeps have +15% health per era, but every kill pays +20% gold per era. Each tower belongs to an era, shown on its button.
Civilization tab: every run earns Science (waves reached and bosses beaten), Production (kills) and Culture (waves where nothing leaked, plus a map clear), more on harder difficulties. Spend them on technologies that unlock towers, boost damage, range, gold and lives, or start every run in a later era. Technologies are saved on this device and are off in Developer mode and puzzles.
Heat map: when a game ends, the board shows where creeps died (blue = few, red = most). The Heat map button or H switches it on and off.
Difficulty: Easy is the baseline. Each level changes creep health, your towers' damage and fire rate, and your gold. Middle: creeps +65% health, towers +5% damage and +5% fire rate, +10% start gold and kill gold. Hard: +165% health, towers +6% / +6%, +25% start gold, +50% kill gold. Extreme: +260% health, towers +12% / +12%, +50% start gold, double kill gold.
Developer mode (Mode menu) adds a Dev tab: edit tower numbers (price, damage, range, reload, radius, buffs, air/ground), creep health, speed and reward, the damage percentages, game settings, and write your own wave script. Press Run to restart the map with your numbers. Edits are saved in the browser, can be exported and imported as JSON, and are ignored in the other modes.
Speed: the Speed button (or F) cycles the game through 1x, 2x and 3x, handy for long runs. Spawners (1 to 4, header menu): every entrance sends that many creeps for each creep in the wave and the stream is faster, but each kill pays less gold (100%, 75%, 67%, 62%). Changing it restarts the map.
Mode: Extended (default) lets you choose what each upgrade improves and set targeting and creep filters. Normal is the simple version: one Upgrade button that adds power and range, and every tower hits every creep type. Changing the mode restarts the map.
Each upgrade takes the tower offline for a short time. Each wave gives you 25 s; when the timer runs out the next wave starts on its own. You can launch the next wave early.
Waves come in blocks of 9: eight regular waves, then a boss wave. In the first block each wave brings one creep type (Normal, Fast, Group, Air, Tough, Decoy, Spawner) and the 8th wave mixes them all. Each later block mixes one more type per wave: two types in the second block, three in the third, and so on. A boss wave has only the boss. Bosses come in turn: Normal on wave 9, Fast on 18, Group on 27, Air on 36, Tough on 45, Decoy on 54, and Spawner on 63, the last wave. Bosses pay a lot of gold.
Keys: 1-0 select a tower (the first ten), with a tower inspected, U upgrades its damage, I its range and O its reload, Esc clears the selection, Space launches the next wave, P pauses and resumes. You can place and sell towers while paused.
